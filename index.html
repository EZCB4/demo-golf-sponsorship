The model

Clubs get signage.
Sponsors get seen.

Every tee on your course is a quiet piece of media. A golfer stands beside it for two or three minutes, eighteen times a round, with nothing else asking for their attention.

We design and install premium tee signs for the club at no cost, and fund them by matching each hole with one carefully chosen local business. The club gets a sharper course. The sponsor gets the most patient audience in advertising. We look after both sides of the deal.

How it works →

From survey to unveiling

Five steps. Nothing for the club to do.

Course survey

We walk all eighteen, photograph every tee and agree positions with the greenkeeper, not over their head.

Sign design

Hole layouts, yardages and club branding drawn up properly and approved by the club before anything is made.

Sponsor matching

We approach local businesses the members already use. The club sees and approves every name before it goes up.

Manufacture

Hardwood or composite, built for wet winters and baking summers, with panels that swap without tools.

Install and maintain

Fitted in a day around play. We inspect twice a season and fix weather damage at our cost, not the club's.
